# 🌍 A Future at Risk – UNICEF Data Analysis

This project explores global patterns in **school infrastructure** and **adolescent health**, using data provided by UNICEF.

The report highlights the connection between **access to improved drinking water in schools** and **AIDS-related deaths among adolescent girls aged 10–19**.

1. **Scatterplot** – Water Access vs AIDS Death Rate
2. **Bar Chart** – Bottom 15 Countries by School Water Access
3. **Time Series** – AIDS Death Rate Over Time (Selected Countries)
4. **World Map** – Global Water Access in Schools

## 🧠 Insights

- Countries with **low water access** in schools often show **higher AIDS death rates**
- Strong need for **cross-sector policy efforts** — education and health are tightly linked
